# Paulie's Phone Gatekeeper - Interaction Design

## Core Service Overview
Paulie's AI-powered crew handles all phone-based business operations:
- Inbound call management
- Appointment scheduling  
- Collections and billing
- Sales and lead qualification
- Personal and professional services

## Interactive Components

### 1. Service Selector & Quote Calculator
**Location**: Main services section
**Functionality**: 
- Toggle between personal/business services
- Select specific services needed (calls, scheduling, collections, sales)
- Dynamic pricing estimate based on call volume and services
- "Get Your Personal Quote" button leads to contact form

### 2. Paulie's Crew Member Profiles
**Location**: Team section
**Functionality**:
- Interactive cards showing different AI assistants
- Hover effects reveal specializations (sales, scheduling, collections)
- Click to see detailed capabilities and success metrics
- Each crew member has unique mobster-inspired name and personality

### 3. Business Hours & Availability Dashboard
**Location**: Contact section
**Functionality**:
- Real-time availability status
- Service coverage hours display
- Call volume capacity indicator
- "Paulie's Available Now" status light

### 4. Contact Form with Service Integration
**Location**: Contact/Quote section  
**Functionality**:
- Multi-step form: Service selection → Business details → Contact info
- Smart field validation with mobster-themed messaging
- Service recommendations based on selections
- "Paulie will call you back" confirmation

## User Journey
1. Land on hero with Paulie's commanding presence
2. See immediate value proposition about saving TIME
3. Explore services through interactive selector
4. Meet Paulie's crew and their specializations  
5. Get instant quote estimate
6. Submit contact form for consultation
7. Receive confirmation that Paulie's team will handle everything

## Key Interactions
- Smooth scrolling between sections with mobster-movie transitions
- Hover effects on all clickable elements with dark theme styling
- Form validation with friendly but authoritative messaging
- Service calculator with real-time updates
- Crew member cards with flip animations revealing details
